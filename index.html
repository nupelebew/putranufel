( 07 ) Process

From brief to posted.

The same five stages, whether it's a product drop or a public service update. I handle each one myself, so nothing gets lost between shoot and edit.

STEP 1

Brief & concept

Agree on the message, the audience, and when it needs to go out.

STEP 2

Shoot on location

Camera, lighting, and sound captured on site.

STEP 3

Edit & grade

Cut for pacing, colour grade, and clean up the audio.

STEP 4

Design the feed

Matching posts and graphics so the account looks consistent.

STEP 5

Publish

Posted on the schedule set with the client.
